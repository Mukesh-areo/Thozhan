Private platform value is not automatically government loss

Tamil Nadu does not have an audited public total for every mobility app. The figures below are transparent scenarios, not proof of tax evasion or State revenue loss.

Pros, risks and safeguards

Pros

Transparent fares, driver retention, Tamil-first service, street-hail meter and better policy data.

Risks

Low-fee viability, adoption, GPS accuracy, privacy, procurement speed and bike-service legality.

Safeguards

Phased pilot, certified distance logic, DPDP compliance, CERT-In review, 24×7 control room and independent audit.

A responsible path to launch

Validate

8–12 weeks, fare engine, security and legal review with 500 drivers.

Pilot

4–6 months in one Chennai zone with 5,000–10,000 auto drivers.

Expand

City growth only after evidence on completion, complaints and cost.

Scale

State rollout after DPR, budget approval and independent audit.
