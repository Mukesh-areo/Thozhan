# Competitor desk research

Research snapshot: 3 November 2026. This records the initial public-source survey discussed with the owner. It is not an exhaustive hands-on audit. Availability changes by city, service and account. Revalidate before using these claims in an external proposal.

| Platform | Publicly advertised capabilities reviewed | Product implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rapido | Bike/auto/cab, customer/captain apps, tracking/sharing, auto/cab subscription terms | Clear offers, easy booking and transparent charges are baseline; subscription pricing is not unique |
| Ola | Auto/bike/cab, rentals/outstation, scheduling, OTP, SOS, contacts, parcels and payment options | Separate service-specific workflows, fare rules and operating procedures |
| Uber | Reserve, rentals, intercity, selected group/parcel/transit/shuttle services and advanced safety preferences | Reservation reliability, flexible trips and proactive safety should be evaluated in the roadmap |

- Linked original order: https://www.stationeryprinting.tn.gov.in/extraordinary/2026/466_Ex_II_2_2026.pdf

The reproduced Gazette identifies G.O. Ms. No. 366 of 27 October 2026 and matches the figures in the supplied plan. The government-hosted PDF could not be fetched during research. Obtain the authoritative order and clarify implementation/rounding rules before billing. The prototype's fare engine is not legally validated.

Review state adoption/licensing, vehicle permits, insurance, payment flows, applicable language requirements, privacy commencement dates and government procurement separately. A national guideline or media report is not a licence to operate.

## Proposed differentiation to test

Transparent fares, driver take-home earnings, reliable pickups, Tamil accessibility, accountable support, transit connections and privacy controls. These are hypotheses for pilot evaluation, not unsupported claims that competitors lack them.

Interview drivers and riders locally; measure willingness to pay, earnings after expenses, cancellation causes, support experiences and accessibility needs. Do not use app-store anecdotes as representative statistics.
