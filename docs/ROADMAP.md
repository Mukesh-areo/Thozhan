# Product roadmap

This is proposed scope, not a claim that these features are complete. Full competitor parity needs a continuing city-specific workflow audit.

| Stage | Scope | Acceptance gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Driver/rider interviews, exact tariff order, legal eligibility, vendor quotations, service area | Approved requirements, cost model and legal go/no-go |
| Core pilot | Secure rider/driver accounts, verification, real routing, auto quotes, dispatch, tracking, receipts | Successful staging trips and server-enforced concurrency/accounting tests |
| Safety and finance | Staffed incidents, masked contact, UPI, refunds, reconciliation, audit, privacy rights | Security, finance and operating sign-off |
| Controlled Chennai pilot | Proposed 50–150 verified drivers in a bounded zone | Measured reliability, driver outcomes, complaints and economics |
| Expanded mobility | Cabs, scheduled dispatch, airport, rentals, multi-stop, intercity | Separate pricing, permits and service operations validated |
| Enterprise and public access | Fleet portal, corporate bookings, cost centres, assisted booking, accessibility | Actual partner supply, accessibility testing and permissions |
| Conditional services | Parcels, bikes, pooling, transit ticketing integrations | Explicit category approval and partner contracts |

## Feature inventory

Passenger: phone login, Tamil/English and other required languages, locations and landmarks, saved places, upfront breakdown, vehicle selection, ETA, tracking, masked contact, OTP, payment, receipts, ratings, cancellation, refunds, lost items, account deletion.

Driver: onboarding, document validation/expiry, availability, offers, navigation, trip states, earnings, transparent fees, payment reconciliation, support, safety reports, suspension appeals.

Operations: onboarding reviews, zones, dispatch monitoring, controlled intervention, tariff versioning, refunds, fraud, complaints, permissions and audit.

Safety: incident queue, severity, responsibility, evidence restrictions, escalation, response metrics, tested procedures and route anomaly handling.

Fleet/enterprise: authorised driver and vehicle management, employee policies, booking for another adult, billing, cost centres and reports.

Government: aggregate coverage, fulfilment, fare compliance, complaint outcomes and pilot evaluation. Personal-data access requires a specific lawful basis and contract.

## Proposed pilot measures

Record fulfilment, time to match, pickup accuracy, cancellations, 4-week driver retention, rider repeat use, audited fare correctness, support response, driver net earnings and per-trip cost. Define thresholds before pilot operation. Do not promise citywide coverage or zero cancellations without evidence.
