Proposal to the Government of Tamil Nadu

Every ride app serves its platform. Thozhan serves the Government's tariff.

தோழன் · நம் நகரம். நம் மக்கள். நம் பயணம்.

Thozhan is one Tamil-first platform for autos, bike taxis, cabs and parcel delivery, built around one thing no app offers today: a digital fare meter controlled by the Government. It applies the Government's tariff for each service, runs on street-hail trips as well as app bookings, and gives the passenger and the driver the same receipt.

What's new

What no ride app does today

Today's ride and delivery apps set their own prices, cover only trips booked through them, and keep the trip data. Thozhan turns the fare meter into public infrastructure that the Government controls, for every service.

The central feature

A digital fare meter the Government controls

The Government sets the tariff for each service; Thozhan applies it to every trip, booked in the app or hailed on the street. It records the tariff, distance, waiting time and fare rules, and gives the passenger and the driver the same receipt. Choose a service and try it:

  • One meter for autos, bike taxis, cabs and parcels
  • Works for app bookings and street-hail trips
  • Tariff changes are made centrally, effective from the date in the order
  • GPS distance measured on the phone and checked on the server
  • Passengers can report an overcharge from the receipt

Who benefits

Good for the Government, passengers and drivers

For the Government

Tariff policy that is enforced

The Government's fare for each service is applied automatically on every trip. A new order is configured once and takes effect statewide.

Privacy-safe mobility data

Aggregated demand, trip times, fare accuracy and complaint statistics for CUMTA planning, with no personal details shared.

Support for gig workers

Transparent earnings records that can help with welfare-board registration and benefits.

Faster emergency response

SOS with trip, vehicle and location details, ready to connect to 112 and the police if approved.

Possible revenue

A share of platform charges, if a public-private arrangement is agreed. See the planning calculator below.

Audit and accountability

Every fare, fee and admin action is recorded with an audit trail that the Government can inspect.

For passengers

A fair meter on the street

Stopped an auto on the road? Start the meter and pay the Government fare, with no bargaining.

₹

The Government's fare, not the company's

Every trip uses the notified tariff, so the price can't change because of a company's demand pricing.

Proof in hand

An itemised receipt that quotes the Government order: the same one the driver sees.

Overcharging you can prove

Report an overcharge straight from the receipt, measured against the official fare.

Also included: SOS, 112 / 181 shortcuts, trip OTP, trusted contacts and Tamil and English screens.

For drivers

The Government's fare backs you

Fares come from the official tariff, not from an app company, so no one can cut the rate on a driver.

No more arguments

The passenger sees the same receipt, quoting the G.O., which settles fare disputes on the spot.

Street trips count too

Hailed trips are metered and recorded, not just app bookings, so all of a driver's work is on record.

Records the Government recognises

Earnings records that can be linked to Gig Workers Welfare Board registration and benefits.

Also included: a flat ₹10 fee on working days instead of commission, document expiry reminders, SOS, and optional insurance through a licensed insurer.

Safeguards built in

  • Driver documents and vehicle verified before going online
  • Trip OTP and number-plate check before every ride
  • Bike taxis: rider safety checklist and helmet confirmation
  • Parcel declaration and photos at pickup and delivery
  • Personal data minimised; passengers can download or delete their data
  • Complete audit trail of fares, fees and admin decisions
Proposed pilot

Start small, measure honestly, then decide

A bounded Chennai auto pilot, with results published against an agreed baseline before any expansion.

Phase 0

Approvals

Nodal officer, tariff and licence clarity, security review, driver consultation.

Phase 1

Closed pilot

50–150 verified auto drivers in one service area, with staffed support and incident handling.

Phase 2

Measure and publish

Fare accuracy, pickup times, driver take-home pay, complaints resolved, accessibility.

Phase 3

Expand if approved

Bike taxis, cabs and parcels, and more areas, only after approval and an independent review.

Government support needed

What we respectfully request

Thozhan cannot succeed as a private app alone. A fair public meter needs the Government's guidance and backing.

CUMTA

Guidance on the pilot area, integration with public transport, and use of aggregated mobility data.

Transport and Road Safety Department

Confirmation of how the notified auto tariff applies to the digital meter, aggregator requirements, and policy for bike and cab services.

Transport Commissionerate

A process to verify driver licences, permits and vehicle documents.

TNeGA

Hosting, data-protection and security-audit guidance, and possible e-governance integration.

Labour Welfare and Gig Workers Welfare Board

Linking drivers' earnings records to welfare registration and benefits.

Finance Department

Review of the ₹10 platform charge, revenue-share options and the pilot budget.

Police and emergency response

Connecting the SOS feature to 112 and women's helpline 181 workflows.

A nodal officer

One point of contact to coordinate departments and approve a defined pilot.

Transparency

Current limitations

  • Not yet in public service. The app has been tested on a test server and a real phone, not with live passengers.
  • Approvals pending. Bike, scooter, cab and parcel fares are proposals until approved.
  • Tariff details to confirm. Rounding and night-charge rules need confirmation from the Department.
  • Figures are scenarios. Revenue and savings numbers are planning arithmetic, not guaranteed outcomes.
  • Insurance would be offered only through a licensed insurer.
  • Before launch: security audit, production hosting, SMS registration and staffed support are still required.
